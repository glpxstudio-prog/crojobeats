Behind the board

Cannis Rojo.

Every artist deserves a room where the idea is safe enough to become a record.

Cannis Rojo is a producer, recording engineer and mix/master engineer based in Tampa, working across Orlando and travelling further when the record calls for it. Fifty-one tracks carry his name so far: Latin, R&B, trap and drill, most of it in Spanish, for artists moving between Florida and Puerto Rico.

He produced and engineered Keyrith's album Nebula from first take to final master, and has worked with JayR, Rubi Mar, Yezielmusic, Lunaticks and FatBoyLyrik, among others. He writes and releases his own music too, under the same name.

The studio is built to be one room instead of four: tracking, production, mixing and mastering, all in the same place. Signed or independent, the work is the same. An engineer in the room who actually listens, and a finish line you can reach.
