What we do

The services.

Walk in with an idea. Walk out with a release.

  1. 01

    Recording sessions

    Tracking with an engineer in the room. Comping, tuning and vocal production are part of the session, not a separate invoice.

  2. 02

    Exclusive beats

    Original production built around your voice, or license something from the catalog. Trackouts and stems delivered either way.

  3. 03

    Mixing

    Balance, depth and space. Two revision rounds as standard, so the record lands the way you actually hear it in your head.

  4. 04

    Mastering

    Loudness and translation across streaming, car systems and club. DSP-ready deliverables with the metadata sorted.

  5. 05

    Photo & content

    Social shoots and session content, so the rollout is ready the same day the record is.

  6. 06

    Release support

    Artwork specs, metadata, distribution prep and a rollout plan. The unglamorous part that decides how the drop goes.

The room

The studio.

Every artist deserves a room where the idea is safe enough to become a record.

Cannis Rojo is a producer, recording engineer and mix/master engineer working out of Wesley Chapel, Florida. Fifty-one tracks carry his name so far — Latin, R&B, trap and drill, most of it in Spanish, for artists moving between Florida and Puerto Rico.

He produced and engineered Keyrith's album Nebula from first take to final master, and has worked with JayR, Rubi Mar, Yezielmusic, Lunaticks and FatBoyLyrik, among others. He writes and releases his own music too, under the same name.

The studio is built to be one room instead of four: tracking, production, mixing, mastering and the content to launch the record. Signed or independent, the work is the same — an engineer in the room who actually listens, and a finish line you can reach.

— Cannis Rojo
